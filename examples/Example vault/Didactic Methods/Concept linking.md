# Concept linking

Linking each note to the shared ideas it uses, so related material connects.

## Why it helps
Connected ideas are easier to retrieve and to apply in new situations than isolated facts.

## Where this vault uses it
"Concepts used" and the concept notes' "Seen in" lists, visible in backlinks and the graph.

## Try it
Open a concept note and follow two of its "Seen in" links. What do they have in common?

## How well supported
Research on concept mapping (for example Nesbit and Adesope's 2006 meta-analysis) shows moderate benefits. Linked notes are a lighter form of the same idea. This is a plain summary of the research, not a literature review; ask for sources if you want to read further.
