# Elaboration

Connecting new material to what you already know and to your own situation: why is this true, where would I use it?

## Why it helps
Links to existing knowledge give the new idea more cues to be found by later.

## Where this vault uses it
The 2-3 questions in "In my life".

## Try it
Answer one "In my life" question out loud with a real example from this week.

## How well supported
Elaborative interrogation and self-explanation were rated moderate utility in Dunlosky and colleagues' 2013 review of study techniques. They help most when you already know something about the topic. This is a plain summary of the research, not a literature review; ask for sources if you want to read further.
