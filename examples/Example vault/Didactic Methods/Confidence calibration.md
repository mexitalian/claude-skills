# Confidence calibration

Rating how well you actually know something, and checking that rating against your answers.

## Why it helps
People tend to overrate what feels familiar. Marking what you knew, half-knew or missed shows where to spend review time.

## Where this vault uses it
The 🟢 🟡 🔴 marks in "Check yourself" and the `confidence` rating, which sets the next review: 1 = +1 day, 2 = +3 days, 3 = +7 days, 4 = +21 days, computed from `last_reviewed`.

## Try it
Before opening a toggle, say how sure you are. Then see whether you were right.

## How well supported
Research on metacognition shows judgments of learning are often off, and improve with practice and feedback. The rating ladder here is a practical rule, not a research-derived schedule. This is a plain summary of the research, not a literature review; ask for sources if you want to read further.
