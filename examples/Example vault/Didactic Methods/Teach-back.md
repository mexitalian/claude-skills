# Teach-back

Explaining the idea simply, as if to someone new to it, without looking (also called the Feynman technique).

## Why it helps
Explaining exposes gaps that re-reading hides, and forces you to organise the idea in your own words.

## Where this vault uses it
The empty "Teach it back" box: three sentences, no peeking.

## Try it
Explain today's idea in a voice note of under a minute.

## How well supported
Studies of learning by teaching (for example Fiorella and Mayer, 2013) show benefits, largest when you actually explain rather than only prepare to. The Feynman label is popular shorthand, not a tested method. This is a plain summary of the research, not a literature review; ask for sources if you want to read further.
