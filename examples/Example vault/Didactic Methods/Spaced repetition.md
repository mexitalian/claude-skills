# Spaced repetition

Reviewing at growing intervals instead of all at once.

## Why it helps
Coming back just as you start to forget makes the memory last longer than cramming does.

## Where this vault uses it
The Cards (scheduled by the Spaced Repetition plugin) and the computed review dates: rating 1 = +1 day, 2 = +3 days, 3 = +7 days, 4 (still solid on a repeat review) = +21 days. The date is computed from `confidence` and `last_reviewed`, never stored.

## Try it
Open the Review Queue, do only what is due today, and rate each one.

## How well supported
One of the most robust findings in learning research (the spacing effect), for example Cepeda and colleagues' 2006 meta-analysis. Rated high utility by Dunlosky and colleagues (2013). This is a plain summary of the research, not a literature review; ask for sources if you want to read further.
