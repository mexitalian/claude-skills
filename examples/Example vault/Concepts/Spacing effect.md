# Spacing effect

Memories last longer when study is spread over time than when it is massed into one session.

## Why it matters for me
- The same study time goes further.

## Example
- Four half-hour sessions over a week instead of one two-hour session.

## Seen in
- [[How spaced practice works]]
- [[W1-01 Welcome and how to study]]

## Not to be confused with
- Interleaving (mixing topics within one session).
