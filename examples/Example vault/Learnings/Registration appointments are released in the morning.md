# Registration appointments are released in the morning

New appointment slots appear on the booking portal at 8:00; by mid-morning the next weeks are gone.

## How it actually works
- The portal releases new slots each morning, a few weeks ahead.
- Other offices in the same city can be booked; they all accept the same forms.

## Gotchas
- The nearest office is usually the most booked. A further one inside the deadline is often the only option.
