# Pretesting

Trying to answer questions about something before you learn it, even when you get them wrong.

## Why it helps
A wrong guess still primes you: it shows you what you don't know and makes the real answer stand out when it arrives.

## Where this vault uses it
The "Before you read" box at the top of every study note: 1-2 questions and a "My answer:" line.

## Try it
Before the next chapter, write one line answering its title as a question. Then read and compare.

## How well supported
Well replicated in lab and classroom studies (the pretesting effect), for example Richland, Kornell and Kao (2009). Works best when the correct answer follows soon after. This is a plain summary of the research, not a literature review; ask for sources if you want to read further.
