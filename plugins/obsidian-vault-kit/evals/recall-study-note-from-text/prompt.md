My Obsidian vault is in the folder `vault`. Make a study note from the text below so I can actually learn it, and save it as `vault/Notes/The two-minute rule.md`.


The two-minute rule says that if a task will take less than two minutes, you do it now instead of writing it down. Writing it on a list and coming back to it later takes longer than just doing it. The rule only applies when you are already working through your inbox or notes; it is not a reason to interrupt focused work. Anything that takes longer than two minutes gets a next action on your list. People often stretch the rule to five or ten minutes, which slowly fills the day with small jobs, so the limit is worth keeping short.
